SECTION 1: STRUCTURED EXERCISE PLANS (WORKOUT SPLITS)
======================================================================

PLAN NAME: Full Body Split
- Best Used For: Beginners, busy individuals, and overall fat loss.
- Weekly Frequency: 3 days per week (e.g., Monday, Wednesday, Friday).
- How It Works: Trains every major muscle group in a single session.
- Primary Purpose: Maximizes workout frequency per muscle group and boosts weekly calorie burn.

PLAN NAME: Push / Pull / Legs (PPL)
- Best Used For: Intermediate to advanced lifters looking to build muscle size.
- Weekly Frequency: 3 to 6 days per week.
- How It Works: Splits workouts by movement patterns. 
  * Push Day: Chest, shoulders, and triceps.
  * Pull Day: Back, biceps, and rear delts.
  * Legs Day: Quads, hamstrings, glutes, and calves.
- Primary Purpose: Allows high training volume for specific muscles while giving them 48-72 hours of total rest.

PLAN NAME: Upper / Lower Split
- Best Used For: People wanting a balance of strength and muscle growth.
- Weekly Frequency: 4 days per week (2 upper days, 2 lower days).
- How It Works: Focuses entirely on the upper body one day, and entirely on the lower body the next.
- Primary Purpose: Provides an ideal balance of high intensity and sufficient recovery.

SECTION 2: INDIVIDUAL EXERCISE MOVEMENT DIRECTORY
======================================================================

MOVEMENT: Barbell Back Squat
- Category: Lower Body (Compound)
- Target Muscles: Quadriceps, Glutes, Adductors, Core.
- Primary Purpose: Building raw lower body strength, functional leg power, and leg muscle mass.

MOVEMENT: Conventional Deadlift
- Category: Posterior Chain (Compound)
- Target Muscles: Hamstrings, Glutes, Spinal Erectors, Latissimus Dorsi, Traps.
- Primary Purpose: Developing total-body pulling power, strengthening the lower back, and improving hip extension.

MOVEMENT: Flat Barbell Bench Press
- Category: Upper Body Push (Compound)
- Target Muscles: Pectoralis Major (Chest), Anterior Deltoids (Front Shoulders), Triceps.
- Primary Purpose: Increasing upper body pushing power and building chest mass.

MOVEMENT: Pull-Up / Chin-Up
- Category: Upper Body Pull (Compound)
- Target Muscles: Latissimus Dorsi (Lats), Rhomboids, Biceps, Core.
- Primary Purpose: Developing upper body pulling strength and creating a wider, "V-taper" back appearance.

MOVEMENT: Overhead Barbell Press (Military Press)
- Category: Upper Body Push (Compound)
- Target Muscles: Deltoids (Shoulders), Triceps, Upper Chest, Core.
- Primary Purpose: Building vertical pushing strength and stabilizing the shoulder girdle.

MOVEMENT: Romanian Deadlift (RDL)
- Category: Posterior Chain (Isolation/Focus)
- Target Muscles: Hamstrings, Glutes.
- Primary Purpose: Specifically targeting hip hinge mechanics to stretch and strengthen the back of the legs.

MOVEMENT: Barbell Bicep Curl
- Category: Upper Body Pull (Isolation)
- Target Muscles: Biceps Brachii.
- Primary Purpose: Isolating the front of the arm to directly trigger bicep muscle growth.

MOVEMENT: Overhead Dumbbell Tricep Extension
- Category: Upper Body Push (Isolation)
- Target Muscles: Triceps Brachii (Long Head).
- Primary Purpose: Isolating and fully stretching the triceps to build arm thickness.

MOVEMENT: Hanging Leg Raise
- Category: Core
- Target Muscles: Rectus Abdominis (Abs), Hip Flexors.
- Primary Purpose: Improving core stability, spinal control, and developing visible abdominal definition.
